Join the Team

We’re building Solution, a unique 2D story-driven game where charm meets dread. To make it happen, we’re looking for passionate creators who want to shape something new with us.

What We’re Looking For

  • 2D Artists with experience in lineart and character design
  • Unity Developers with clean code and understanding of design patterns
  • Animators with a flair for expressive storytelling

Who Can Apply?

Anyone with the right skills, passion for games, and willingness to collaborate. Whether you’re a student, hobbyist, or experienced professional - if you love what you do, you’re welcome here.

Important Note on Collaboration

This is currently an unpaid collaboration. Payment will only be possible if we secure sufficient funding. By joining, you should be aware of the risk: if the game doesn’t succeed, you may not receive payment.

Instead, treat this as a learning experience, a way to grow your skills, and a chance to boost your visibility in the indie game dev scene.
