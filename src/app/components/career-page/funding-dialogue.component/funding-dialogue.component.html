Funding Distribution Framework

This document is an initial conceptual plan and is not a legally binding contract. Terms may change as development and finances evolve.

This document outlines the initial conceptual plan for how external funding will be distributed within the Solution team. The structure below is a guideline; individual contracts may contain legally binding terms beyond this framework.

Note: This framework does not define post-release profit sharing in full, but the revenue model is expected to follow a similar logic.

1. Royalty Allocation (10%)

Ten percent (10%) of total monthly funding is reserved for royalty payouts. That pool is split equally among valid royalty contracts, regardless of individual royalty rates.

Royalty entitlement is influenced by two factors:

  • Unpaid Active Development Days (pre-funding)
  • Approved Team Member Investments

1.1 Contribution Credits (“Active Days”)

To reward early unpaid work, qualifying team members receive $10 per Active Day.

Definition of an Active Day
  • A commit to the project repository was made on that calendar day, and
  • At least five days within the surrounding 7-day window (3 days before and 3 days after) also contain commits.

This rule favors sustained development rather than isolated commits. Exceptional contributions may be reviewed by the Team Lead for additional credit.

1.2 Investments by Team Members

Team members may make personal contributions (for example, hiring a freelancer or buying an asset) provided:

  • The investment is pre-approved by the Team Lead (Kirils Turkins)
  • The expense is documented and aligns with project goals

Approved investments are eligible for repayment at a 120% rate through the royalty pool. Example: a $20 approved investment returns $24.

2. Reserve Fund Allocation (30%)

Thirty percent (30%) of monthly funding is directed to the Company Investment Fund (the central reserve), managed by the legal entity holding project finances.

  • Purpose: fund development, licenses, infrastructure, and related side-projects
  • Restriction: Not for personal use of the project owner

3. Departmental Budget Allocations (60% total)

The remaining 60% is split across four departments. Department-level percentages below determine each department's monthly budget; internal distribution within departments is handled by contracts and agreements with respective leads.

  • Development Department: 15%
  • Visual Department: 20%
  • Audio Department: 20%
  • Marketing & Community Department: 5%

3.1 Development (15%)

Funds programming staff, tooling, and third-party services.

3.2 Visual (20%)

Funds 2D artists, animators, asset purchases, and contracted visual work.

3.3 Audio (20%)

Funds composers, sound designers, voice actors, and possible royalty holdbacks for licensed audio.

3.4 Marketing & Community (5%)

Funds advertising, community events, press kits, merchandising samples, and community moderation costs.

4. Summary of Monthly Funding Allocation

Category Percentage Primary Use
Royalty Allocation 10 % Credits to contributors (Active Days & Investments)
Reserve Fund (Company) 30 % Investments, operational overhead
Development Department 15 % Programming, tooling, third-party services
Visual Department 20 % Art salaries, asset purchases, outsourced art
Audio Department 20 % Composers, sound designers, voice actor fees
Marketing & Community Dept. 5 % Advertising, events, promotional assets
Total 100 %

5. Governance & Change Control

  • Periodic Review: Quarterly Funding Council reviews spending vs. budget.
  • Amendments: Changes require majority vote of all four departments and the Team Lead.
  • Transparency: Monthly financial reports (receipts, invoices, balances) are shared with active team members.
